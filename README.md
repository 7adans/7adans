# Hi, I'm Ladino Anselmo 👋

**Senior Mobile Engineer (Flutter · Swift/SwiftUI). I build the apps that companies ship to the App Store and Google Play, and I own them from architecture to release.**

Most mobile apps don't fail on launch day. They fail six months later, when every new feature breaks two old ones, nobody trusts the tests, and releases become something the team is scared of.

I've spent 6+ years making sure that doesn't happen. At **Oderço**, one of Brazil's largest tech distributors (35+ years in the market), I architected, built and published both of the company's e-commerce apps on iOS and Android, as the end-to-end owner:

- 🛒 **Oderço**: the official B2B e-commerce app, a mobile-first alternative to the web platform ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/oderco/id6741322311) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.br.oderco.app))
- ☀️ **ODEX**: e-commerce for photovoltaic equipment, built for solar integrators ([App Store](https://apple.co/411j5HP) · [Google Play](https://bit.ly/3Q5kw1P))

Before that, I designed and shipped my own product end-to-end: **Super Vaga**, published on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/mz/developer/ladino-anselmo-arnaldo/id1591706007) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=5684520659897537418), with its .NET 6 + DDD backend ([source](https://github.com/7adans/SupervagaApi_BasicsDDD)).
